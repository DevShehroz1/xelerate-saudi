OUR SERVICES

Interior Detailing

Deep Clean, Refined Feel

A Cabin That Feels Brand New

The interior of your vehicle is where you spend every drive — and it deserves the same level of care as the outside. Xelerate's interior detailing service is a thorough, systematic restoration of every surface inside your car: seats, carpets, dashboard, center console, door panels, glass, and headliner. We use professional steam cleaning, pH-balanced shampoos, and dedicated leather care products to dissolve years of embedded dirt, stains, and odors without damaging a single material.

Beyond aesthetics, a properly detailed interior maintains the value of your vehicle and creates a healthier environment for every journey. Our technicians pay attention to the details other services overlook — air vents, seat-track rails, stitching lines, and weatherstripping. When we hand your keys back, your cabin will smell fresh, look immaculate, and feel as refined as the day it left the factory.

What's Included

Every Surface, Restored

Vacuum & Blow-Out

A powerful vacuum and compressed air blow-out removes loose debris from every crevice — under seats, in door pockets, through vents, and along floor-rail channels.

Seat & Carpet Shampooing

Fabric seats and carpets are treated with a professional hot-water extraction shampoo that lifts embedded soiling, food stains, and discolouration from the fibres.

Leather Cleaning & Conditioning

Leather surfaces are cleaned with a pH-neutral leather cleaner to remove grime and body oils, then conditioned to restore suppleness and prevent long-term cracking or fading.

Dashboard & Console Wipe-Down

All hard surfaces — dashboard, center console, door cards, and trim panels — are thoroughly cleaned and dressed to restore a factory-fresh, non-greasy appearance.

Glass Interior Cleaning

All interior glass surfaces are cleaned with an ammonia-free glass cleaner and buffed streak-free, eliminating haze and film that reduce visibility and give a cloudy appearance.

Odor Elimination Treatment

An ozone or enzyme-based odor treatment neutralises cigarette smoke, food, and pet odors at the molecular level — not just masking them with fragrance.

Our Process

How We Work

01

Vacuuming

We begin with a full vacuum and blow-out of the entire cabin, clearing all loose debris before any wet products are applied to any surface.

02

Deep Clean

Surfaces are cleaned with the appropriate product for each material — steam for hard surfaces, shampoo extraction for fabric, and dedicated cleaners for leather.

03

Conditioning

Leather is conditioned, plastic trim is dressed, and fabric is treated with a fabric protector to repel future stains and keep surfaces looking fresh longer.

04

Final Inspection

We perform a detailed walkthrough under bright lighting to verify every surface is clean, dry, and correctly treated before the vehicle is returned to you.
