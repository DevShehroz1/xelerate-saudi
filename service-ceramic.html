Ceramic Coating

Hydrophobic Shield, Lasting Gloss

Protection That Bonds for Years

A molecular-bond coating that repels water, blocks UV, and resists chemical contamination — lasting years, not weeks.

Every application begins with full decontamination and correction so zero imperfections are locked under the coating.

What's Included

A Complete Coating Package

Paint Decontamination

A full chemical and clay-bar decontamination removes iron fallout, tar, and bonded contaminants, leaving the surface perfectly clean and ready for polishing.

Paint Correction

Light to moderate machine polishing removes swirl marks and surface defects so the coating bonds over a flawless finish, maximising depth and reflectivity.

Surface Prep & IPA Wipe-Down

The paintwork is wiped down with an isopropyl alcohol solution to strip all polish residue and surface oils, ensuring a completely clean bonding surface for the coating.

Ceramic Coating Application

Professional-grade ceramic coating is hand-applied panel by panel using dedicated applicator pads, ensuring uniform, streak-free coverage across the entire vehicle.

Flash & Cure

The coating is allowed to flash to the surface under controlled studio conditions before being levelled with a microfiber cloth, initiating the cross-linking curing process.

Hydrophobic Topper Coat

A final ceramic-infused topper layer is applied to boost water-beading behaviour, add slickness, and enhance the depth of gloss immediately after the base coating cures.
