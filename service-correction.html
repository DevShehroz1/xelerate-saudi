Paint Correction

Flawless Finish, Zero Defects

Defects Removed. Gloss Restored.

Swirl marks, scratches, water spots, and oxidation removed by machine — not masked. True paint clarity restored.

We measure paint thickness first, then work through compounding, polishing, and finishing under LED inspection lights at every step.

What's Included

A Complete Correction Process

Paint Thickness Measurement

All panels are measured with a digital paint depth gauge before polishing begins, establishing safe working limits and identifying any areas with previous repairs or thin clear coat.

Machine Polishing (Stage 1)

A heavy compound paired with a cutting pad removes deeper scratches, water spots, and heavy oxidation in the first aggressive polishing stage, rapidly reducing the defect level.

Machine Polishing (Stage 2)

A medium polish with a lighter pad refines the surface after compounding, removing any haze or micro-marring left by Stage 1 and building clarity and gloss depth.

Finishing Polish

A fine finishing polish with a soft foam pad perfects the surface, eliminating the last traces of holograms and producing the maximum achievable gloss and clarity in the paint.

Paint Inspection under Lighting

Every panel is inspected under dedicated LED correction lighting to verify that all defects have been removed and no new marring has been introduced during the polishing process.

IPA Wipe-Down

The paint is wiped down with isopropyl alcohol after correction to remove all polish oils, revealing the true corrected finish and preparing the surface for any coating or sealant.
