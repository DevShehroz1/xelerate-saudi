OUR SERVICES

Exterior Detailing

Perfecting Every Surface

Showroom Finish, Every Time

Your vehicle's exterior is the first thing the world sees — and at Xelerate, we make sure it leaves an impression. Our professional exterior detailing service is a comprehensive, multi-stage process that addresses every surface of your car: paint, glass, wheels, tires, and trim. Using industry-grade chemicals and hand-application techniques, we strip away months of road grime, mineral deposits, and embedded contaminants to reveal the true depth and clarity of your paint.

Unlike an automated car wash that scratches as much as it cleans, every step at Xelerate is performed by hand in our climate-controlled studio. We follow a strict decontamination-first protocol — clay bar treatment, iron fallout removal, and pH-balanced washes — before applying a durable paint sealant that locks in the gloss and shields your finish from the harsh Saudi climate. The result is a vehicle that looks and performs as if it just rolled off the showroom floor.

What's Included

Everything Your Exterior Deserves

Hand Wash & Dry

A thorough two-bucket hand wash using pH-neutral shampoo, followed by a careful microfiber drying process to eliminate water spots and micro-scratches.

Wheel & Tire Cleaning

Dedicated wheel cleaner and soft brushes remove brake dust, road tar, and grime from rims and wheel wells. Tires are dressed for a clean, lasting finish.

Paint Decontamination

Chemical decontamination with a dedicated iron fallout remover dissolves embedded metallic particles and road contaminants that washing alone cannot remove.

Clay Bar Treatment

A professional-grade clay bar glides across the paint surface, lifting bonded industrial fallout, tree sap, and overspray to leave the paint perfectly smooth to the touch.

Paint Sealant Application

A synthetic polymer sealant is hand-applied to the entire painted surface, providing months of UV protection, hydrophobic water behaviour, and a deep, reflective gloss.

Exterior Trim Dressing

All rubber seals, unpainted plastic trim, and exterior moldings are cleaned and conditioned with a dedicated trim restorer to prevent fading and cracking.
